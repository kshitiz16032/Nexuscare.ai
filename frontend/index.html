Clinical Foundation & Technical Methodology

The Dataset (Pima Indian Cohort)

The predictive model is calibrated using the National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases database. The cohort consists of female patients of Pima Indian heritage. Features include clinical metrics such as Glucose tolerance, Diastolic blood pressure, BMI, and Insulin levels to predict risk outcomes.

SHAP Mathematical Explainability

Unlike standard black-box machine learning engines, LucidClinics AI implements cooperative game theory Shapley values. Every parameter is assigned an additive contribution score, representing how much that specific metric shifts the individual prediction from the baseline expected value of the training set.

Guidelines Retrieval (RAG)

Upon identifying the patient's primary risk driver, the backend performs a semantic vector search on ChromaDB. The system calculates cosine similarity embeddings using all-MiniLM-L6-v2 to retrieve targeted guidelines from the American Diabetes Association (ADA).
