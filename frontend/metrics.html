Algorithm Calibration

LucidClinics AI runs an optimized XGBoost (Extreme Gradient Boosting) classifier. The training sequence applies Stratified K-Fold cross-validation to maintain demographic ratios. Under the hood, the training engine addresses sample skew by weighting positive indicators proportionately using class frequency coefficients (scale_pos_weight).
